Cursor recorder

Ground truth for the synthetic cursor, from your own hand. Pick a mode, use the board with a real mouse, then download both files. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is rounded.

Mode

  1. Game: play a real game against the weak engine, and think as long as you like. This is the mode that matters: the pauses have to be YOURS, not a pause you were told to take. Click-click or drag, both are recorded. Promotion is always a queen, because the choice is a dialog and a dialog is not a hand.
  2. Targets: click the lit square, then the next one. Keep going for about three minutes, which is roughly 150 clicks.
  3. Free play: move pieces around however you like, click-click and drag both, for about two minutes.
  4. Then download both files. Twenty or more trials per distance band is where the percentiles stop moving.
