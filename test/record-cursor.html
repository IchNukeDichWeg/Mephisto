Cursor recorder

Ground truth for the synthetic cursor, from your own hand. Pick a mode, use the board with a real mouse, then download both files. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is rounded.

Mode

  1. Targets: click the lit square, then the next one. Keep going for about three minutes, which is roughly 150 clicks.
  2. Free play: move pieces around however you like, click-click and drag both, for about two minutes.
  3. Then download both files. Twenty or more trials per distance band is where the percentiles stop moving.
